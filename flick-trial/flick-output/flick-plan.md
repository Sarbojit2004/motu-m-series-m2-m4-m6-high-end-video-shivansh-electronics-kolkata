## Format

9:16 portrait — **1080 × 1920 at 30 fps**. Total 178.000 s = 5,340 frames.
Every scene is an independently registered Remotion composition; the 25 are concatenated into the single master.


## Brand assets

All **30** unique images in `brand-assets/images/` (see `IMAGE-INDEX.md`), plus both logos.
**25 of the 30 are landscape-oriented (AR 1.40–4.41)** and are scaled complete to the frame width with a
deliberate fill behind them. None is cropped. Full palette, type system and the 180/220/64 caption-safe
zone are in `BRAND-GUIDE.md`.


## Creative direction

Light ground for the whole runtime. Camera vocabulary is this pipeline's own: Gimbal Micro-Movement on
every still, Macro-to-Full-Reveal reserved for the three front panels, Interface Sequence on the LCD,
Port Density Sweep on the three rear panels, and scale-and-fill for every wide frame.

**Every one of the 30 images appears, complete and uncropped.** Screen time per image is short — that is
accepted and wanted; the reel should feel fast and dynamic. What is not accepted is filler: each image,
however brief, gets real framing, an intentional piece of motion, and a considered cut in and out.

Both logos are drawn plain, never boxed. Shivansh Electronics appears far more often than MOTU, and
`www.shivanshelectronics.in` is the single most-repeated element. All three MOPs are stated distinctly.
No other audio-interface brand is named. No background music — this project layers its own afterwards.
