# MOTU M-Series — real-world deployment films

Two deliverables, one project, one set of B-rolls.

| | Composition | Size | Length | Outro |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical reel | `Reel` | 2160 x 3840 | ~90 s | 6 s |
| Landscape explainer | `Explainer` | 3840 x 2160 | ~300 s | 10 s |
| Reel cover | `ThumbnailReel` | 2160 x 3840 | still | — |
| Explainer cover | `ThumbnailVideo` | 3840 x 2160 | still | — |

## Hard constraints

1. **No pricing.** No figure, no currency, no "starting at", no comparison of
   cost, in either film, in either thumbnail, in any caption. Not once.
2. **No branding outside the outro.** No Shivansh logo, no MOTU logo, no
   website, no phone number, no watermark, no corner bug, anywhere in the body
   of either film. The picture is the product and the work, nothing else.
3. **The outro is the only branded frame.** 6 s on the reel, 10 s on the
   explainer. It carries, and only it carries:
   - Shivansh Electronics logo
   - MOTU logo
   - the designation, verbatim:
     **Shivansh Electronics is the exclusive distributor of MOTU
     (Mark of the Unicorn, USA) for East and North-East India**
   - the website
   - all three contact numbers
4. **Nothing is reused from the earlier M-Series work.** Not the script, not the
   shot plan, not the research, not the reels. This is a new editorial system
   built on real-world deployment footage.

## The editorial system (carried from the AVB series and the UltraLite-mk5/828)

* full-bleed imagery — nothing letterboxed, nothing pillarboxed, no ground
  showing through
* burned-in captions overlapping the picture, the whole typographic layer at
  `TYPE_OPACITY = 0.64`, legibility bought back with a hard tight drop shadow
  rather than a scrim
* the three-tier caption lockup split at one key word
* gimbal camera language throughout — dolly, orbit, push, rack focus
* **no image is ever cropped to a diagonal, sliced, or shown partially**

## The B-rolls

Ten clips, ten seconds each, one set serving both films:

* **Explainer** — every clip plays in full, all ten seconds.
* **Reel** — the main section of each clip, cut to the beat of the script.

Generated at 1080p (not 4K) deliberately: 4K generation credits are not worth
it when the clip is graded, punched and overlaid before it reaches the master.

## Audio

* **Music bed** — the bed built for the AVB series and the UltraLite-mk5/828.
* **Transition cues** — the palette cut for the Soundcraft Signature Plus films.
* Everything mastered to one reference so every source plays at unity.

## Delivery

4K masters, uncompressed from the render — no second encode to fit a limit.
Where a master exceeds GitHub's 100 MB ceiling it ships as playable segments
stream-copied from that master, never as a re-encode.
