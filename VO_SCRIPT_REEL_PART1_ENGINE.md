## Tone: Warm & Trustworthy, with the spec authority of Precise & Technical-but-Accessible

Drawn from the creative brief's Section 9. I've blended the first two profiles and deliberately left out **Cinematic & Aspirational**.

**Why.** Part 1 exists to do one job: retire the buyer's "am I settling for less?" anxiety, which the brief names as the M2 buyer's central frustration — the "anxiety of settling", exacerbated by impostor syndrome. That is a *reassurance* problem, and reassurance is what the Warm & Trustworthy voice does: a knowledgeable studio mentor leaning over a desk, conversational and steady.

But reassurance alone would sound like hedging when the whole claim rests on four hard numbers — ESS Sabre32 Ultra, 120 dB, −129 dBu, 2.5 ms. So the spec moments borrow the Precise profile's crisp enunciation: those figures are stated flatly and confidently, with no upward inflection and no salesmanship. Warmth carries the argument; precision carries the evidence.

Cinematic & Aspirational is wrong here specifically because it treats recording as an emotional milestone. That register suits an opener, not an 88-second technical explanation, and at this pace it would read as overselling the smallest unit in the range — the exact impression this reel must avoid.

**Delivery notes**
- **Pace: 140–150 wpm.** Deliberate, matching the reel's unhurried cut rate. This script is written *under* budget on purpose — the visuals need room, and there are four vector diagrams that should be allowed to land in silence.
- **Never sell the M2 as a compromise.** No "even the M2", no "just two channels", no "entry-level". The framing throughout is *same instrument, smallest scale*.
- Let the spec numbers sit. A short beat after each figure.
- The scene at 39–47 s (the metering reveal) and the diagram at 60–67 s are intentionally light on words. Do not fill them.

## Compliance notes for whoever records this

- **The distributor designation** appears in full, unabbreviated, as on-screen text in the outro and again mid-reel at 74–80 s: *"Shivansh Electronics is the Authorized Distributor of MOTU (Mark of the Unicorn, USA) Interfaces for East and North East India."* Line 15 speaks only the company name because the full designation is legible on screen for 9.7 seconds; if you would rather it be spoken in full, take it in place of line 14's tail and shift the continuation line to 82.0.
- **No other interface manufacturer** is named, compared to, or implied anywhere in this script.
- **Pricing** is always "Market Operating Price", incl. GST. The M2 figure — ₹26,900 — is the only price in Part 1; the M4 and M6 figures belong to Part 2.
- **No line implies the M2 or M4 sound inferior to the M6.** Lines 6 and 11 are the load-bearing ones; keep their wording.
- Every specification spoken here is marked VERIFIED in the creative brief's Section 4 master table.
