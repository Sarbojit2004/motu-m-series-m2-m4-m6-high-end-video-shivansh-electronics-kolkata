# Voiceover script — MOTU M-Series portrait reel (Flick trial)

**Deliverable:** `flick-trial/out/motu-m-series-portrait-flick-trial.mp4` — 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 5,340 frames, 178.000 s
**Language:** English only.
**Tone:** Warm & Trustworthy, with the spec callouts read in the Precise & Technical register.

Same tone as this project's prior M-Series builds, and still the right fit: the whole narrative
exists to remove the "am I settling?" anxiety, which is a mentor's job rather than a narrator's.
Cinematic & Aspirational would oversell a desktop interface and undercut the plain-fact spec
claims; pure Precise & Technical would read as a lecture and leave the emotional problem in the
opening unanswered. Both tones are drawn from the AVB reference's established set.

**This script was written FIRST and handed to Flick as its source material.** Unlike every prior
script in this project — each written against an already-planned video — Flick plans its scenes
*from this text*, so the timings below are the reel's actual scene boundaries, not an annotation
added afterwards. It is stored verbatim as `flick-trial/script-for-flick.txt` and as the
`segments` array of `flick-trial/flick-output/transcript.json`.

**Pace:** 454 words over 178 s = 153 wpm overall; no line exceeds 175 wpm.

**Recording:** no burned-in captions. The music bed is mixed to leave headroom, so a voice
tracked around −16 dBFS sits cleanly above it.

## Constraints held

- All three MOPs stated **distinctly** — Rs. 26,900 / Rs. 32,900 / Rs. 55,900 — once inside each
  product's own segment, and all three together at **[02:45.000 – 02:53.000]**. Never rounded,
  never blended into one figure or a range.
- The best-price direction to **www.shivanshelectronics.in** is spoken in that same beat,
  alongside the three figures rather than instead of them.
- The close carries the positioning line; the exact unabbreviated designation — *Authorized
  Distributor of MOTU (Mark of the Unicorn, USA) Interfaces for East and North East India* —
  is rendered on screen.
- No comparison to any other audio-interface brand. No mention of TASCAM.
- Only verified figures are spoken. Preamp gain range is never stated, because the source
  documentation does not verify it.
- The M6's four combo inputs are described as **rear**-mounted, which the supplied photography confirms.
