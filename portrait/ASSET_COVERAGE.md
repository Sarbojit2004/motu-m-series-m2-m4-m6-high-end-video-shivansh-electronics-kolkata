## Wide-aspect treatment — FIT + FILL, never cropped

19 of the 30 are landscape-oriented (AR 1.40-3.45). Each is scaled **complete** to the frame
width and the remaining height filled with a blurred, dimmed copy of the same image — a
colour field pulled from its own palette. The complete image is always the subject; nothing
is cropped to fit, and nothing is dropped for being the wrong shape.
